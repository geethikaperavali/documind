Responsible AI & Usage Governance

AI Safety Framework
Developer & System Responsibility
  • Strict Data Isolation: Document text and vector embeddings are segregated by user ID with Row Level Security (RLS). Never shared or used to train public foundation models.
  • Evidence-Backed Grounding: RAG generation requires verbatim chunk citations and confidence scoring. Queries with insufficient context decline to speculate.
  • Multi-Source Provenance: Research and comparative audits cite primary government, legal, and academic registries with publication timestamps.
User & Operator Responsibility
  • Human-in-the-Loop Verification: AI recommendations, WBS plans, and checklists are decision-support drafts. All outputs must be reviewed and approved by human experts before consequential action.
  • Non-Guaranteed Correctness: AI models can misinterpret edge cases or generate probabilistic answers. Never treat AI outputs as certified legal counsel or definitive compliance validation.
  • Source Inspection: Users are advised to inspect primary document citations and external source links before finalizing business decisions.
Intended Purpose: DocuMind is an AI intelligence and decision-support workspace designed to accelerate document comprehension, research synthesis, and comparative analysis. It is not an autonomous legal representative, licensed medical advisor, or fiduciary financial consultant.
